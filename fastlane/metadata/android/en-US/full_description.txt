<p><b>readest-tiny</b> is a local-first ebook reader and library with optional self-hosted sync.</p>
<p>Read EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and other supported formats with flexible reading modes, bookmarks, and customisable reading settings. Local reading works without an account.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Multiple eBook formats:</b> Read EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW, AZW3, FB2, FBZ, CBZ, TXT, and Markdown.</li>
<li><b>Flexible reading modes:</b> Switch between scrolling and paginated layouts, with fixed-layout zoom and page-fit options.</li>
<li><b>Book navigation:</b> Use a table of contents, full-text search, progress navigation, and reading history.</li>
<li><b>Bookmarks:</b> Add, rename, jump to, and remove bookmarks; sign in to sync them.</li>
<li><b>Personalized reading:</b> Customize fonts, themes, colors, page layout, headers, footers, and page animations.</li>
<li><b>Local-first library:</b> Import books, search your shelf, track progress, and manage local copies without an account.</li>
<li><b>Optional self-hosted sync:</b> Sync book files, covers, metadata, progress, and bookmarks through a configured backend.</li>
</ul>
